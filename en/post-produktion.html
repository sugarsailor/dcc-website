Post-production is where the film comes alive.

Even the best footage needs professional post-production to reach its full potential. Color grading, precise editing and thoughtful sound design turn good raw material into emotional content that resonates.

As a post-production specialist based in Vorarlberg, I work with DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects – and I also offer remote post for agencies and production companies looking to outsource capacity.
