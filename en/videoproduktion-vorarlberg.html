Frequently asked questions from Vorarlberg

Yes – I work throughout Vorarlberg and am also available for projects in the Lake Constance region (Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg, eastern Switzerland). For larger projects I also travel to Vienna, Graz or Innsbruck.

What sets you apart from local video production companies?

I combine traditional video production with modern AI workflows. That means faster production, more content variations and significantly lower costs at consistently high quality. As a one-person business without agency overhead, I can also work more flexibly and directly.

Do you also work remotely?

Yes – post-production, motion design, 3D and AI content workflows run fully remotely. For video shoots I am happy to come on site.

How quickly can you start a project?

For smaller projects, often within a few days. Larger productions we plan together – typically with 2–4 weeks of lead time for shooting days.
